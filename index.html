How we work

Every post follows the same four steps.

Pick

We choose only topics that many people are already talking about or searching for.

Check

Facts and numbers are checked against the original reports before writing.

Design

Each slide is designed in a vertical 4:5 or 9:16 layout for easy reading on a phone.

Publish

Finished posts are scheduled and published to our own Instagram and TikTok accounts.

Our publishing tool

To post on time every day, Shortime uses a small in-house publishing tool. It is used only by the Shortime team, only with Shortime's own accounts. There is no public sign-up and no login on this website.

🔗

Connect

The account owner connects Shortime's own TikTok account once through TikTok Login Kit (scopes: user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload).

🖼️

Upload

Finished photo posts and videos made by Shortime are sent through the TikTok Content Posting API, with a caption and privacy setting chosen by the owner.

✅

Confirm

The tool checks the post status and records the result. Nothing is posted that the owner has not created and reviewed.
